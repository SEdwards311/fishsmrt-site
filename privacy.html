FishSMRT Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026. FishSMRT (“the app”) is a saltwater fishing and boating planner for the Tampa Bay area, operated by Stephen Edwards (“we”). Questions: contact@edwardsag.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and a password (stored securely by our database provider; we never see your password in readable form).
  • Your fishing data: catches (species, length, bait, notes, time), fishing spots you save, photos you add, weather and tide conditions looked up for each catch, and plans you save from Ask the Captain.
  • Photo details: when you pick a photo, the app reads its date, time and GPS location (if the photo has them) to fill in the catch. This happens on your phone; the photo and the catch details are saved to your account.
  • Location: if you allow it, your phone’s location is used while the app is open to show you on the map and to place a spot. It is not tracked in the background and is not saved unless you save a spot or catch.
  • Questions to the Captain: the questions you type and the answers.
  • Feedback: if you use “Send feedback,” we store your note, the screen you were on, and your app and iOS version.
  • Usage counts: how many AI questions and photo identifications you used each day (to apply daily limits), and anonymous token counts to measure cost.

How we use it

To run the app: show your logbook and map, build trip plans, identify fish in photos, answer your questions, and keep your account secure. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

Who else handles your data

  • Supabase hosts our database, photo storage and sign-in. Your data is stored there and protected so that only your own account can read it.
  • Anthropic (the maker of Claude) processes the text of your questions, your logbook summary sent with a question, and photos you ask the app to identify, in order to produce answers. Under Anthropic’s commercial terms, this data is not used to train its models.
  • Weather and tide services (NOAA, the National Weather Service, Open-Meteo) receive the approximate place and time of a forecast or catch to return conditions. They do not receive your name or email.

The app’s operator can technically access data stored in the database for support and maintenance. We do not look at your personal data except to fix a problem you report or as required by law.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: in the app, tap the line at the bottom of the screen (your email, “Account”) and choose “Delete my account.” This permanently deletes your logbook, photos, spots, saved plans and sign-in. It cannot be undone.
  • Delete individual items: you can delete catches, spots and saved plans inside the app.
  • Location and photos: you can turn these off in iPhone Settings. The app still works without them.
  • To ask what we hold about you, or to request deletion by email, write to contact@edwardsag.com.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it or delete your account. Saved plans delete themselves 14 days after the trip date unless you mark them “Never delete.”

Important notes

  • FishSMRT is a planning aid. It is not for navigation. Fishing rules shown in the app come from the Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission but can change; always confirm at myfwc.com. Depths, coordinates and AI answers can be wrong.
  • The app is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
